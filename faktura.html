Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

FAKTURA Publisher is an internal content management application for FAKTURA, a bilingual food media project.

1. Information We Process

The application may process Pinterest account identifiers, board information, Pins, publishing metadata, and analytics made available through authorized Pinterest API access.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to prepare, schedule, publish, manage, and analyze FAKTURA's own content on Pinterest.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the application or comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application data and access credentials are protected using appropriate technical and organizational measures. Access is restricted to authorized operators.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. Authorized users may request deletion of stored information by contacting the project operator.

6. Third-Party Services

The application uses Pinterest API services and may use automation infrastructure to manage publishing. Pinterest's own privacy practices are governed by its separate privacy policy.

7. Your Rights

Where applicable, individuals may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the project operator.

8. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact FAKTURA through its official social media profile.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our application or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page.
